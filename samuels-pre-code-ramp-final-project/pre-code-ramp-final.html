The Wonderful World of Technology!

In todays day and age, technology is everywhere and I mean EVERYWHERE!

Every where you go, the influence of technology is felt all around.

  • Gadgets and Machinery
  • Software and Applications
  • Weaponry and Medical Usage
  • ...and regular everyday appliances!

Technology advances more and more everyday, some may even question whether or not we are losing some of our humanity through constant exposure to tech.

Whatever some may think, whatever opinions some may have about the ethics of technology and how it affects our humanity... One thing is for sure, most humans can no longer live without technology, and I think it's for the best. The convenience and accessibility to information available online has helped the world in a multitude of ways.

Technology has given us the wonderful ability to do things or learn things that once took years of study or experience within a short time frame. It has also allowed us to see things that once were deemed impossible like the idea of holograms or being able to go to space! We as humans are even in the process of creating commercially available flying vehicles!
